About Helix

Celebrate yourresplendence.

Helix Salon is the brainchild of Sam Ploetz, a master stylist and curly girl. She wanted an inclusive, feminist space for all genders and curl types, so she built a curl-centric and nerdy/geeky salon.

Our space is private and quiet, a relaxing get-away from your every day. Mask-friendly, too: feel free to mask, and we'll mask if you'd like us to.
