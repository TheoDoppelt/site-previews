At the bench

One cobbler. Real tools. Shoes you already love.

Jim's Cobbler Shoppe is Magnolia's neighborhood shoe repair, a few doors into the village on 32nd Ave W. Jim does the work himself at the bench: heel caps, soles, torn stitching, broken hardware, faded leather.

Bring in the boots you broke in years ago, the sandals you wore all summer, or the bag with a strap hanging by a thread. Fixing them almost always beats starting over.

Good to know
  • Walk in, Tuesday through Saturday, 9 to 5:30.
  • Closed Sunday and Monday.
  • Not sure it can be saved? Call and ask Jim.

Repairs · claim ticket in hand

What comes across the counter

Every job gets a ticket. Here's what Jim fixes most.

  1. No. 001

    Heels & heel caps

    Worn-down heels rebuilt and new caps put on, so you stop walking on the nails.

  2. No. 002

    Soles

    Sole repair and protectors to stretch the life of good shoes.

  3. No. 003

    Stitching

    Torn seams re-sewn, including hiking and work boots.

  4. No. 004

    Hardware

    Buckles, rivets and lace loops replaced when they give out.

  5. No. 005

    Leather touch-up

    Torn or scuffed leather glued down, dyed and brought back.

  6. No. 006

    Sandals

    Straps, footbeds and soles on summer favorites, Birkenstocks included.

  7. No. 007

    Bags, belts & packs

    Straps, seams and stitching on purses, belts and backpacks.

Something else? If it's leather or it goes on your feet, ask.
