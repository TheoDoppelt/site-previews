Our story

The neighborhood's bookstore for over thirty years

Magnolia's Bookstore is an independent general bookstore in the heart of Magnolia Village, serving the neighborhood since 1992.

The shop is small on purpose. Every shelf is chosen with care, so there's something good wherever you look without it ever feeling like too much. Our booksellers know the stock, read widely, and are happy to help you find the next one, whether it's a gift, a book club pick or something for a long weekend.
