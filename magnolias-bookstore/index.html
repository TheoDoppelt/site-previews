About the store

1992

Magnolia's Bookstore has been the neighborhood's general bookstore for more than thirty years. It's an independent shop in the heart of Magnolia Village, stocked with a carefully chosen mix of books for adults, teens and kids.

The space is small on purpose. Every shelf is picked so there's something good wherever you look, without it ever feeling like too much. The booksellers know the stock, read widely, and love helping you find the next one, whether it's a gift, a book club pick or something for a long weekend.

Ask at the counter

  • Special orders and holds

    Don't see it? Call or email and we'll order it in, usually within a day or two, or set aside a copy for you.

    Every book you buy here can go home wrapped and ready to give.

  • Gift certificates

    Any amount you like. Buy one by phone, or online through Bookshop.org.

    Regulars earn rewards as they read. Just ask about it at checkout.
