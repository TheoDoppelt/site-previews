Raffi got his start as a jeweler and master diamond setter in Beirut, Lebanon, where he was born and raised, training under renowned Lebanese jewelers. The city's history, luxury and natural beauty still show in his designs.

Twenty years later he's creating one-of-a-kind pieces with global influences in the Pacific Northwest, from a shop in one of downtown Seattle's historic buildings.
