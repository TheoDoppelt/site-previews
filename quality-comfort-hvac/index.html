FAQ

Common questions

How often should my system be serviced?

Once a year is a good rule — ideally in early fall for heating systems. Heat pumps that run year-round benefit from a check each spring and fall.

How often should I change my filter?

Every 1–3 months for standard 1-inch filters, more often with pets or during wildfire smoke season. Thicker media filters can last 6–12 months.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes — we install, maintain, and repair heating and cooling for both residential and commercial properties.

My house has no ducts. Can I still get AC?

Yes. Ductless mini-split heat pumps mount on the wall and need only a small hole to connect to the outdoor unit. They heat and cool.
