The studio

Phinney Ridge, since 2001

Lambright Design Group opened in January 2001 and has worked mostly on houses ever since, from single-room remodels to new homes across Seattle's neighborhoods.

Every project starts with two questions: what do you actually want from this house, and what design gets you there? From there you stay part of the process through design and construction, so you understand each decision as your home takes shape.

Small on purpose: you work directly with the person drawing your house.

two 1960s houses
Sheet A-104

View Ridge Mid-Century Remodel

The owner wanted fresh interiors that still felt true to the house's 1960s modernism. We opened the kitchen and family room to each other and set a clerestory monitor at the center of the roof, which pulls daylight into an east-facing house. Downstairs became an easy room for family gatherings.

Sheet A-103

Seward Park Mid-Century Remodel

We kept the original 1960s structure and reworked the living area so the rooms connect better and frame the views outside. Shared and private spaces were rethought, and folding door systems replaced many exterior walls, so the living room opens to the yard on both sides.
