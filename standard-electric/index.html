Difficult older homes are the everyday job here.

Plenty of electricians can wire a new build. Fewer want to fish new circuits through an older Queen Anne house without opening up half the walls. That's the work Standard Electric has specialized in for decades: bringing older homes up to modern, inspected wiring while protecting the rest of the house.
