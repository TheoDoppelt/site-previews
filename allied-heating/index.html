Repair or replace? We'll tell you honestly.

The most common question we get. Here's how we think about it.

Usually worth repairing

  • The system is still fairly young
  • It's one part, not a pattern of breakdowns
  • Your energy bills haven't crept up

Time to consider replacing

  • Repairs keep stacking up year after year
  • Rooms are uneven or it never quite keeps up
  • You want efficient heating and cooling in one system
