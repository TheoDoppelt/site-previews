About Tyler

"I should've brought my own knives."

Tyler spent years in professional kitchens and learned how much the tools at hand shape the work. Every friend's or family event, every knife he picked up was dull. That's what started him sharpening other people's knives. He has a passion for a sharp edge and customer satisfaction.

Set upa time.

No fixed shop hours. Just call, text or email and Tyler will set something up. Mobile service is available depending on volume and distance.
