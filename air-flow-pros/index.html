Why it takes more than a shop vac

A hand-held vacuum reaches the first foot of a duct. The Hornet is a truck-mounted power vac: it puts the whole system under suction, and the dust goes out a hose to the machine instead of back into your hallway.

A shop vac

  • Reaches what you can see
  • Small hose, small motor
  • Dust can blow back in

The Hornet

  • Pulls the whole system
  • Truck-mounted suction
  • Dust leaves the house
