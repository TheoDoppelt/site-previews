Know whatto bring in.

Every item needs a deposit at drop-off, and it covers the first block of bench time. If it's a simple fix, that's all you pay.

  • Simple repairs: fixed within the first service time, with parts under $25 pre-approved, so no delay.
  • Bigger jobs: parts over $25 or more time, we send a detailed estimate first. Approve or decline.
  • Additional labor: $120/hr, billed in 15-minute increments.
