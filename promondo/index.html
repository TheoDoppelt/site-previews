About

Two brothers who grew up here

Building things is our passion. We founded Promondo to take part in rebuilding modern-day Seattle, and we're grateful for every project that lets us do it.

JM

Joe Monda

Co-owner

Born and raised on Capitol Hill, Joe studied history at Seattle University. That led him to Seattle's landmark buildings, including the Loveless Building, where he was manager and head of maintenance. His interest in preserving property grew into Promondo, a maintenance and remodel business for downtown's new entrepreneurs and for Seattle homes.

MM

Matt "Mateo" Monda

Co-owner

After graduating from the University of Washington, Mateo founded Mondo Caffé in Guadalajara, where he became known as a pioneer of gourmet coffee in Western Mexico. He handled concept, design, construction and upkeep of every café and restaurant he opened. After selling the business, he came home to Seattle and joined Joe to grow Promondo.
