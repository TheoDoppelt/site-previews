Drop off

Plan on leaving your dog for about four hours (cats, about two). We'll give you a pickup time, and you can always call to check in.

Comfy kennel

A spacious kennel with water at all times, supervised, and kennel time kept as short as possible.

Bath, nails, ears

Every bath and haircut includes a nail trim, ear cleaning and anal glands. Cats are blow-dried and finish with a cool kennel dryer made for pets.

"We like to see everyone shiny, fluffy and happy when they leave." Every six to eight weeks keeps coats healthy.
