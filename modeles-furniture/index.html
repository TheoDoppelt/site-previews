The majority of our merchandise comes from individual homes where the furniture has been gently used. New pieces arrive almost daily, so you're sure to see something new each time you visit. Alongside the consigned pieces, we keep a wonderful collection of new retail accessories and gifts you can count on finding in stock.

Interested in consignment? Here's how it works.

We preview items to see if they're a good match for the store. One good overall photo of each item is usually enough. Email it to deb@modelesfurniture.com.

  1. 1Send a photo and the details
  2. 2We agree on pricing
  3. 3A written 60-day contract
  4. 4It sells, you get paid
Before day 1

Send us a photo

One good overall image per item, plus:

  • Manufacturer's name
  • Age
  • Where purchased
  • Price paid (invoices help)
  • Overall dimensions
  • Condition: wear, stains, sun-fading, loose joints, water rings

We agree on pricing

Pricing depends on the item, its overall condition and the level of demand. Generally, customers expect to pay no more than half of retail in a consignment setting. Some items go lower, a few can go higher.

A 60-day contract

Once pricing is agreed, you get a written consignment contract for a 60-day period. Delivery to Modele's is at the consignor's expense, but we'll recommend the most cost-efficient way. We're fully responsible for your piece while it's with us.

Sold!

We send you 50% of the sale price within 14 days of the sale. If it hasn't sold in 60 days, we can extend the contract with price reductions, or you can pick it up.
