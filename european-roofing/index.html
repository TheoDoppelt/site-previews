05 — Philippe Pollien

The detail most roofers skip.

Copper, zinc and stainless steel can all be soldered, and Philippe solders the joints on site wherever he can. Soldered connections stay waterproof longer and look cleaner than ones that are only folded or sealed. It's a skill that takes years to learn.
