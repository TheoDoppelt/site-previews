Ask any locksmith three questions.

Lots of "$15 locksmith" listings use local numbers and fake addresses, then send you to a distant call center. An honest shop answers these without hesitating.

1

What's the exact price?

An honest locksmith gives you an exact quote for the service you're asking for.

2

Where are you located?

We're at 2232 NW Market St in Ballard, and have been part of the neighborhood for decades.

3

What's your legal name?
