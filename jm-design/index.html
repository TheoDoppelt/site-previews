Spaces that feel elegant and welcoming at the same time. Beautiful, practical, and easy to live in.

JM Design is a Seattle interior design studio led by Julie Evans Reid, specializing in high-end residential design, new construction and extensive remodels, along with select commercial, multi-family and resort projects.

We work closely with architects, builders and trades, and our planning and clear communication keep your project on track from the first walk-through to the final pillow.

Who we are

The studio

JER

Julie Evans Reid

Principal / Lead Interior Designer

Julie began her career in Seattle in 1983 and has spent more than 40 years in high-end residential design, specializing in new construction and extensive remodels. She has worked across the West Coast, the East Coast and London, and her recent focus on second homes brings luxurious yet inviting retreats to life. Clients and contractors return to her for her meticulous planning and clear communication.

JH

Jess Hupf

Interior Designer

A decade of interior design experience plus a background in social media marketing. A mother of five, Jess designs spaces that are stylish, functional and family-friendly, whether it's a single room or an entire home.

TM

Thomas Miller

Senior Architectural Interior Designer

More than three decades in architecture, planning, interiors and construction. Thomas shapes the architectural direction of projects, from early concepts and 3D modeling to construction and final detailing.
