From one clog-maker to a shop of seven.

Swedish immigrant Bjorn "George" Swanson repaired boots for the U.S. Army in World War I, then opened Progressive Shoe Repair downtown in 1928. In 1947 the shop moved to Wallingford as Swanson's Shoe Repair, where it still stands. Three more generations of Swansons have trained and worked here since.
